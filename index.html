M.Pharm DissertationPharmaceutics

Development, Optimization and Characterization of Extended-Release Floating Pellets of Lurasidone Hydrochloride

Postgraduate research completed at Dadasaheb Balpande College of Pharmacy, Besa, Nagpur, during 2025–2026.

Research focus

Development, optimization and characterization of extended-release floating pellets of Lurasidone Hydrochloride, including formulation and process optimization and evaluation of release and absorption behaviour.

Areas explored

  • Formulated pellets using the extrusion–spheronization technique and coated them using a fluidized bed processor (FBP).
  • Applied Design of Experiments (DoE) for formulation and process optimization.
  • Developed and validated a bioanalytical method using spectrofluorometry.
  • Conducted in-vitro drug release and in-vivo absorption work and assessed in-vitro–in-vivo correlation (IVIVC).
